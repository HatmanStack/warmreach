# Acceptable Use Policy

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Not reviewed by a lawyer. See
> `docs/legal/README.md`.

**Version:** `2026-07-26.1`

WarmReach exists to help you maintain professional relationships you actually
have. It is not a mass-outreach tool, and the limits below are not negotiable
regardless of plan.

## You may not use WarmReach to

**Send bulk unsolicited outreach.** Do not use WarmReach to contact people at
volume with generic messages. The rate limits in the product are a floor, not a
target, and staying under them does not make bulk outreach acceptable.

**Harass, stalk, or intimidate.** Do not use the product to contact someone who
has asked you to stop, to contact someone repeatedly against their wishes, or
to build a profile of someone for the purpose of harassing them.

**Impersonate anyone.** Do not operate a LinkedIn account you are not authorised
to operate, present yourself as someone you are not, or use generated messages
to misrepresent your identity, employer, or relationship to the recipient.

**Scrape LinkedIn at scale for resale or redistribution.** WarmReach is built to give you better access to your own network — the connections you already
have and the data you can already see. Do not repurpose it to compile, sell,
publish or transfer datasets of profile information. The product stores
connection data to serve _you_; it is not a data-collection pipeline, and using
it as one is a breach of these terms.

**Target people who are not your professional contacts.** The product is built
around your existing network. Do not use it to reach people you have no
plausible professional reason to contact.

**Circumvent someone's expressed preference.** If a person has declined a
connection, ignored repeated messages, or told you not to contact them, do not
use automation to keep trying.

**Break the law where you are.** Including but not limited to anti-spam
legislation (such as CAN-SPAM, CASL, PECR), data protection law (such as GDPR,
UK GDPR, CCPA), and employment or recruitment regulation in your jurisdiction.

**Automate an account you do not own** without documented authority from the
account holder.

## Your obligations to the people in your network

WarmReach stores information about your connections, including inferences
generated about them by a language model — estimated relationship strength,
likelihood of replying, influence within your network.

Those people did not agree to this. Depending on where you and they are located,
**you may be a data controller with your own obligations to them**, including
providing notice and honouring their access and deletion requests. Using this
product does not transfer those obligations to us and does not discharge them.

If someone asks you to delete what you hold about them, you are responsible for
doing so. The product's export and deletion features exist to help you comply,
not to comply on your behalf.

## Enforcement

If we become aware of use that breaches this policy we may suspend or terminate
the account, with or without notice depending on severity. We do not owe a
refund for a subscription terminated for a breach of this policy.

We do not proactively monitor the content of your messages. Enforcement is
generally triggered by a report or by an unusual usage pattern.
